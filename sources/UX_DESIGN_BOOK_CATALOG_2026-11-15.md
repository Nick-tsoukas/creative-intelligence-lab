## Evidence conventions
- **V**: direct full-text access page or Gutenberg edition confirmed as a real complete work in this research or confirmed by existing Lab source ledger. Exact rights depend on edition. A full-text link is not a statement that the book has been read.
- **C**: curated *bibliographic research lead only*, not a verified full text, borrowing slot, or permitted download. Titles, author editions and years should be checked before acquisition; dates with “unverified” must remain unverified.
- **READ:** only exact chapter/section stated. Never use FULLY READ unless every part has been checked. For Vignelli and HCI, large PDF reading interfaces may limit visual inspection; full PDF presence does not imply all figures reviewed.
- **Formats:** for Gutenberg works the landing page exposes HTML, EPUB, TXT where supported. Rights descriptions apply to source editions, not later translations.
- **Original-synthesis storage:** links + original notes only in public GitHub, not restricted full-text copies.

## Acquisition verification protocol
1. Resolve exact edition and original author/title from publisher, catalog or original title page.
2. Open the complete full text (not a listing, preview, publisher blurb or research abstract), inspect opening/middle/end and contents. If PDF, inspect page images/figure fidelity.
3. Record stable direct source URL, copyright/public domain jurisdiction, actual downloadable formats, translation/version notes and full vs excerpt.
4. Compare title-author-year keys against both tables and legacy Lab sources. For duplicate works/editions, link editions under one work, not inflated counts.
5. Promote C to V only after these checks; retain dates and reviewer evidence. Prioritize C entries with authorized library borrowing if not open.
6. Keep textbooks, standards/pattern sites, original psychology studies and article series classified separately. **WCAG 2.2, WAI-ARIA APG, GOV.UK Design System, Material, Apple HIG and NN/g heuristics are important but not counted as books.**
