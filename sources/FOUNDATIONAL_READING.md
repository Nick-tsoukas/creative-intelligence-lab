# Foundational Reading and Viewing Queue

**NOT A CLAIM OF COMPLETE READING.** These are research priorities, to be examined using originals when legally available, official publisher material, trusted museum holdings and critical research.

## Composition, perception, color
- Josef Albers — *Interaction of Color* (1963)
- Rudolf Arnheim — *Art and Visual Perception* (1954)
- Josef Müller-Brockmann — *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* (1981)
- Johannes Itten — *The Art of Color* (1961)
- Molly Bang — *Picture This* (1991)
- Rudolf Arnheim — *Visual Thinking* (1969)

## Typography and graphic design
- Ellen Lupton — *Thinking with Type* (2004)
- Robert Bringhurst — *The Elements of Typographic Style* (1992)
- Jan Tschichold — *The New Typography* (1928)
- Paul Rand — *Thoughts on Design* (1947)
- Massimo Vignelli — *The Vignelli Canon* (2009, freely distributed PDF by author)
- Bruno Munari — *Design as Art* (1966)
- Alan Fletcher — *The Art of Looking Sideways* (2001)

## Image, cinema, space and movement
- Bruce Block — *The Visual Story* (2001)
- Richard Williams — *The Animator's Survival Kit* (2001)
- John Berger — *Ways of Seeing* (1972)
- Kevin Lynch — *The Image of the City* (1960)
- Scott McCloud — *Understanding Comics* (1993)
- Don Norman — *The Design of Everyday Things* (1988; revised edition 2013)

## Primary artifact anchors
- Leonardo da Vinci — *The Last Supper*, Museo del Cenacolo Vinciano, https://cenacolovinciano.org/
- Piet Mondrian — *Composition No. I with Red and Blue* (1931), https://www.museothyssen.org/
- Josef Müller-Brockmann — *Beethoven* (1955), Smithsonian collections https://www.si.edu/collections
- Tadao Ando — *Church of the Light* (1989), https://artsandculture.google.com/

## Research rule
For each source: record title/edition/year, reliable link, status, what actually read/viewed, concrete insights and limitations. Never invent quotations or page citations.
