## Research reading process
For each priority book, acquire the text legally where possible. Record exact edition, pages/chapters accessed, quotations only within copyright constraints, core claim in author's own context, examples, conflicting interpretations, and a close-looking exercise. Promote specific lessons to the handbook only after source depth is adequate.

## Current concern
We have far more source listings than full book readings. Address this deliberately; don't let the library become an overconfident bibliography or a collection of unsourced aphorisms.
