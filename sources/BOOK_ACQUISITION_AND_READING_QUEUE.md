## Practical download policy
- Download **public-domain** original editions in full where useful and rights are clearly established, including illustrations rather than low-quality OCR alone.
- Prefer primary institutional scans or publisher-authorized editions and maintain exact edition/translator/pagination records.
- A public-facing web link is not a blanket license to copy a modern translation. Do not commit publisher scans or restricted assets to a public repo.
- Retain only a legal private copy where permitted. Commit **reading dossiers, references and original teaching diagrams**, not unauthorized book content.
- Plain text OCR is not sufficient for visually important plates; inspect page images and compare transcription carefully.
- Book count is not an outcome. Every read section must produce a precise claim, counterexample, visual mechanism, and falsifiable design exercise.
