## 7 core discoveries worth keeping
1. A complete 2025 HCI textbook can anchor current user-research/cognitive/interaction work. This is more broadly useful than adding 100 generic UX listicles.
2. **Detectable is not understandable:** recognize/search/detect are different user problems (HCI Ch3).
3. Components need semantic domain contracts: shared visual styles alone cannot resolve wrong-recipient risks (Frost + Lynch/Horton).
4. Vignelli begins with *meaning* and context before applying grid; creative direction should not be a styling phase detached from task.
5. Vignelli explicitly allows expressive ambiguity yet cautions that it backfires; do not allow ambiguity in audience/privacy actions.
6. Participatory input is evidence, while AI personas and designer inference are not observations of users (Design Justice).
7. Historical psychology supplies questions about learned habits and interest, not authoritative modern neurobiology (James compared to HCI 2025).
