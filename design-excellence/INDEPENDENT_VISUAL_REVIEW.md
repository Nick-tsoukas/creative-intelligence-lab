## 4. Independent critique — no averaged beauty score

- **Concept originality:** distinctive to product/audience or interchangeable with another AI template?
- **Art direction:** typography, composition, photography, visual narrative, deliberate spatial tension and pauses.
- **Human task:** can an unfamiliar user see and complete the task, including recovery?
- **Authenticity:** real artist media and brand identity; no fabricated captions, social proof, stats or documentary claims.
- **Accessibility/performance:** keyboard focus, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, contrast, slower phone, image loading.
- **Build quality:** actual state/permissions truth, maintainable code, no false clickable affordances.
