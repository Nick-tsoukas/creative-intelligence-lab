# Digital Design Trends — Dated Watchlist (2026)

These are **hypotheses worth investigating**, not assertions that the industry has universally adopted them.

- Cinematic editorial typography: expressive large display type, but evaluate legibility and responsiveness.
- Material-like digital imagery: gradients, depth, texture, lighting; distinguish substance from decoration.
- Scroll-driven storytelling: inspect navigation, re-entry, mobile motion, and accessibility.
- Motion systems: design transitions to communicate hierarchy and continuity instead of attracting attention for its own sake.
- Browser-native animation capabilities: evaluate View Transitions and CSS scroll-driven animations versus heavier tooling.
- Interactive 3D/WebGL: justify against actual comprehension, device support, and performance.
- Highly tailored components: bespoke interaction identity balanced against learned conventions.
- Brand-specific visual systems: adapt across marketing, product, email and native devices.
- AI-assisted creative workflows: compare speed, originality, intellectual property issues and consistency.

For each trend: document dated examples, counterexamples, longevity hypothesis, accessibility and performance risks, and evidence beyond popularity. Never conflate fashion and excellence.
