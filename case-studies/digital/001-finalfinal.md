## Problem and documented approach
**FACT, studio account:** FinalFinal is intended to gather music collaborators' files, notes, and versions. Locomotive says the website had two complementary purposes: explain the product and express the creative culture of the people using it. The project's organizing tension is **control vs creative chaos**. The studio describes sound-driven interaction, creator storytelling, cinematic narrative around making a track, and motion as demonstrations of product character, not mere decoration.

The documented team includes art director Bastien Allard, UX designer Laurence Mailloux, UI designer Léo Parpeix and developers Alex Gattefossé and Jérémy Minié.

## Critical visual interpretation (not a tested interaction report)
**INTERPRETATION:** The compelling design problem is *how to communicate disciplined collaboration without making music culture look bureaucratic.* The studio explicitly frames structure and expressive spontaneity as two poles; this is a useful art-direction tension. The more distinctive hypothesis is that showing a creator's process can explain a workflow better than a static feature list.

**What this does NOT establish:** the site's current conversion rate; usability by novices; navigation success; accessibility; animation smoothness; mobile battery use; whether sound is opt-in; or source code/tech stack.

## UX questions and potential failure modes
1. Can a visitor understand what FinalFinal does within seconds, even if sound/motion is disabled?
2. Are story-driven sections skippable and accessible from keyboard?
3. Do artists discover the actual workflow, pricing and next action without completing a cinematic sequence?
4. Does the sound-driven presentation respect user preference and reduced motion?
5. Does the music-culture personality survive smaller screens without hiding essentials?

## Transferable principles
- Choose a tension intrinsic to the audience's work; do not paste a generic creative visual style on the brand.
- Make the experience *demonstrate* product mechanics, but also offer direct textual explanation.
- Treat sound and animation as optional expressive layers over understandable content.
- **Do not imitate** this project's distinctive graphics, cinematic sequences or branding.
