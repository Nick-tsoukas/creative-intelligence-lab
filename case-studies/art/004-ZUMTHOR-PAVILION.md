# Art Case Study 004 — Peter Zumthor's 2011 Serpentine Pavilion

**Architect:** Peter Zumthor. **Landscape design:** Piet Oudolf.
**Source:** https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/whats-on/serpentine-gallery-pavilion-2011-peter-zumthor/
**Date reviewed:** 2026-11-11.
**Evidence:** Commissioning institution narrative and architect-authored statement, not first-hand visit.

## Verifiable project
A pavilion organized around an enclosed garden within a garden. Visitors cross from the outside lawn into a built enclosure and eventually into an open-sky contemplative garden. Serpentine publishes construction imagery, watercolors and commissioned photographs.

## Close reading
**Interpretation:** Experience is generated by *threshold*: from surrounding activity to protected interior. Dark enclosure is not an arbitrary stylistic preference; it intensifies the contrast with the planted interior and sky.

## Use in digital storytelling
A landing page could transition from emotional context to an open, clear product workspace; the shift should serve comprehension and never prevent direct navigation. The surrounding environment and destination should be meaningfully connected.

## What would disprove the transfer
If visitors dislike delays, cannot find the product features, or need to bypass the story to complete a task, then the threshold is merely decoration.
