## Three *research directions*, not approved homepage mockups
The following share the same required truth, offer, links and privacy constraints. They are prompts for the Creative Design Director, not designs to deploy:

1. **The Afterglow / cinematic documentary.** Strong authentic human photographic action, remembered event details, restrained dark/warm light interplay. Atlas mechanisms: MA-003 Hopper **only as light threshold (not absent entrance)** and MA-007 Parks for provenance/subject dignity; MA-006 Ando for meaningful reveal with skip.
2. **The Tour Journal / editorial archive.** A sequence of photographs, show details, short artist stories and approachable product proof; intentionally varied content sections. Atlas: MA-001 Vermeer for quiet human detail, MA-008 Reid Miles for information/photo integration. Avoid pretending 1950s jazz design defines all modern bands.
3. **The Artist's Signal / bold typographic culture.** Large custom display language and a consistent, precise operational layer; image/hero scale responds to the musician's verified identity. Atlas: MA-004 Paula Scher, MA-002 Hokusai *abstract* rhythmic scale; avoid copied Public Theater typography and Japanese wave iconography.

All directions must survive authentic very long band names, 320/390px screens, real CTA destinations and reduced motion; preserve approved first-fold compositions unless owner specifically permits changing them. **No unverified endorsements, musicians' photos, conversion statistics, trial/pricing terms or generated pictures passed off as documentaries.**
