## First target: artist Night Recap
The user's known redesign concerns include weak gallery visual hierarchy, overlarge editorial drawers, a frustrating story-editing experience, bland media-source selection, audio/video context, QR/link sharing, and balancing powerful concert visuals with safe editing.

These are **reported design questions**, not independently verified current production defects. Before critique, capture/import a real current-state screenshot and inspect the current branch and UI states. Distinguish event poster (which can contain QR artwork) from an actual shareable Night QR resolver. Do not invent media upload/publish controls or audience/privacy semantics.

Each capture is private, provenance-linked and separately classified as a screenshot or actual interaction test.

### Source finding
Retrieve 3–6 source links for one focused problem: for example inline editorial editing, music-native gallery photography, a genuinely mobile audio upload, or share sheet + URL/QR. Inspect the specific real product before recording a pattern, and avoid copying an app's recognizable branded layout.

### Art direction
The Faneqo North Star remains minimal, expensive, precise, spacious, dark/calm, cinematic, personal and music-native. Protect current approved compositions. Three original directions should differ in **editorial premise, photographic logic, typographic role, spatial sequence and interaction method**, not merely color. Aim for beautiful but usable artist context, with clear public/private, draft/published and retry states.
