## Actual arguments, separated from inferred transfers
**Ch 1: Why and How.** The author distinguishes imitating what a finished work looks like from investigating the maker's *reasons* for choices. He describes alternating near-range craft/action with far-range critique/whole-work purpose. Across media, learning about decisions requires asking both **how** and **why**, not mechanically cloning surface appearance.

**Ch 2: Craft and Beauty.** Making something functional is insufficient to describe the care and beauty that can inhabit a crafted artifact. The author's argument is **aesthetic philosophy and illustrative storytelling**, not a controlled study that proves attractive pages convert better. He ties deep care to an audience's participation and use of the artifact.

## Cross-disciplinary synthesis candidate
Compare Chimero **why→how / near→far** with Dow **compositional relation and original exercises**:
- Agreement as *interpretation*: train judgment by making alternatives and evaluating them against a purpose rather than copying finished work.
- Different emphasis: Chimero prioritizes **purpose, audience, creative iteration and meaning**; Dow prioritizes **line/mass/color organization and progressive exercise**. Neither supplies a method for verifying modern usability without actual observations.
- Product decision: a homepage may be visually original yet misrepresent product purpose; task comprehension and art quality should be critiqued separately, then brought back into dialogue.

## Possible design process (NOT implemented here)
1. Write an evidence-backed sentence about the visitor and their desired outcome (**far / why**).
2. Sketch art-direction variations preserving the same message (**near / how**).
3. Inspect actual screenshots and ask which choice strengthens or weakens that intended communication (**far**).
4. Implement and test keyboard/mobile/performance/task journey (**near**) if authorized.
5. Repeat only when new evidence justifies a change.

## Candidate knowledge cards
- **DK-015A — Purpose before polish.** WHEN a visually impressive concept communicates little, retrieve verified visitor needs and state design objective prior to adding effects; COUNTEREXAMPLE an intentionally expressive cultural artifact may make atmosphere the primary objective. Source: Ch1; applied to digital UX by the Lab (hypothesis).
- **DK-015B — Alternate making and independent critique.** WHEN polished screens keep masking a conceptual error, separate production and whole-experience evaluation; COUNTEREXAMPLE a tiny routine CSS correction doesn't need an art-direction committee. Source: Ch1; applied to UX by the Lab.
- **DK-015C — Beauty beyond utility.** WHEN an experience is functionally sufficient but emotionally generic, inspect craft, expression, context and audience; **do not** trade away truthful controls or accessibility to increase ornament. Source: Ch2, philosophical claim (not empirical evidence).
