## 6. Independent critique after actual rendered output
- **Aesthetic distinction:** does it communicate a meaningful thesis? What is generic or derivative?
- **Cultural truth:** are photos, identity, event detail, testimonials or numbers verified?
- **UX correctness:** can a novice perform the primary job and recover from mistakes?
- **Visual craft:** typography, proportion, image hierarchy, spacing, responsive rhythm.
- **Accessibility and performance:** measured outcomes only where actually run.
- **Owner choice vs model choice:** record who approved what.
- **Revisions:** specific failures with screen/test evidence and next change.

**Evidence status:** aesthetics/UX impact **NOT MEASURED** until independent critique or real user/technical observation. Never claim design superiority from the existence of this packet.
