## 1. Purpose & audience
Who is the work for? What should the visitor understand or do?

## 2. Visual observations
Composition, typography, color/light, imagery, spatial hierarchy, pacing and brand-specific features. Describe actual states, not adjectives alone.

## 3. Interaction and UX
Navigation, user control, feedback, components, forms, keyboard, screen reader (only if tested), mobile, reduced-motion alternatives.

## 4. Engineering and performance
Only state verified technologies and measured performance with method, date and device. Otherwise mark unknown.

## 5. Artistic interpretation
How do choices affect emotion, recognition, and meaning? Separate interpretation from documented designer intent.

## 6. Weaknesses and counterexamples
Where does expression interfere with understanding or task completion?

## 7. Transferable ideas
Underlying principles, conditions of use, and what **not** to imitate.

## 8. Sources
Citation URL, author, publication date, page title, accessed date, status.

## 9. New hypotheses / next experiments
Testable questions arising from observation.
