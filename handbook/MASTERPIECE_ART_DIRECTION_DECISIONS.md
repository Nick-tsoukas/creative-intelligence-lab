# Masterpiece Art Direction — Eight Conditional Reasoning Moves

**Research increment:** 2026-11-15. **Relationship:** an *additive* on-demand module for Creative Intelligence Lab / any more advanced v5.0 system. Existing source files `research/008`, `research/013`, old alpha Creative Design Director skill and the UX Director remain authoritative within their original scopes. **Do not replace an unavailable v5.0 installation or re-install alpha over it.**

Read this guide **only for a genuine visual/art-direction task**, not routine UX bugs. Start with the real product/audience/constraints, select **one or two** relevant ideas and **one or two** actual Masterpiece Atlas studies. Avoid an always-on mega-prompt. Use `python3 scripts/query_masterpiece_atlas.py search "light"` or `show MA-006` to retrieve the chosen original-source card.

Each move below is a **transfer hypothesis** drawn from actual studies, not a law of beauty or experimentally certified design improvement.

## AD01 — Mean something before making it spectacular
**When:** a page looks polished, yet its product/cultural thesis is weak. **Decision:** state what the person should understand, feel and do; then name a real subject-specific tension (transitory/permanent, performer/audience, intimacy/scale). **Example:** Vermeer’s local gesture versus whole-room structure (MA-001); treat the work as an art object with own history, not a template. **Failure:** atmosphere overrules the actual product, pricing or CTA. **Check:** independent visitor first-glance comprehension, real links and honest proof. **Counterexample:** an expressly noninstrumental art installation may prioritize open-ended reading.

## AD02 — Compose using relationships rather than a formula
**When:** a wall of uniformly weighted features feels generic or hides the core action. **Decision:** identify dominant mass, secondary spaces, rhythm and deliberate contrast. Sketch first in black/white before selecting color/animation. **Example:** Hokusai (MA-002) for differing scale and distant reference, and Albers (MA-005) for the limits of color judgment when surrounding fields change. **Failure:** one giant hero steals space from necessary forms, accessibility or equal-priority tasks. **Check:** 320/390px reading path, contrast/focus, genuine content grouping.

## AD03 — Let authentic imagery establish the world
**When:** photos feel like generic decoration or the page claims to serve people without showing their reality. **Decision:** specify *subject, verified context, activity, vantage, light, crop, rights and consent* before image choice. **Example:** Gordon Parks and Ella Watson (MA-007) show how personhood and power matter to framing; Hopper (MA-003) illustrates dramatic spatial light as fiction/atmosphere. **Failure:** copying symbolic adversity, fake artist portraits, unlicensed photos, invented endorsements or staged screenshots passed off as live customer data. **Check:** source/permission ledger and task comprehension; distinguish portrait from proof.

## AD04 — Establish an expressive typography / utility typography contract
**When:** a cultural identity needs distinctive lettering without degrading navigation or forms. **Decision:** allow assertive type for headlines, posters, public editorial scenes; preserve readable, responsive, semantic typography in operational UI. **Examples:** Scher’s Public Theater `Simpatico` poster (MA-004), Reid Miles’s Blue Note sleeve (MA-008). **Failure:** rotate tiny CTA labels, hide legal terms, borrow famous letterforms/identity without rights, rely on tiny text after reflow. **Check:** long real names/copy, zoom, font fallback, accessible labels, mobile typesetting.

## AD05 — Use architectural sequence without trapping a visitor
**When:** a story has several meaningful emotional chapters. **Decision:** express arrival, orientation, proof, exploration and resolution using varied density, space, and one purposeful reveal; preserve direct navigation and skip. **Example:** Tadao Ando’s Church of the Light (MA-006) and its photographed interior/creator account. **Failure:** forced scroll animations, opaque dark initial screens, delayed CTA, sensory discomfort. **Check:** first view, keyboard, reduced motion, real route targets, media-off, slow device.

## AD06 — Distinguish stable brand grammar from expressive campaigns
**When:** a product needs a bold campaign identity without redesigning every utility screen. **Decision:** define persistent tokens/voice/interaction semantics, and vary one or two *expressive dimensions* per public campaign or artist. **Example:** Pentagram's multi-year Public Theater work and Blue Note original label examples (MA-004/MA-008). **Failure:** card soup, different-looking but indistinguishable products, a new brand for each screen, trademark imitation. **Check:** render public expressive page beside inbox/settings; compare shared type, color meaning, spacing and component affordances.

## AD07 — Invent three **premises**, not three skins
**When:** an agent proposes three visual directions that are actually the same grid with new gradients. **Decision:** keep verified copy, user job, audience, primary action and technical constraints constant; vary *narrative thesis, visual center, typographic role, photographic logic, spatial sequence and motion grammar*. **Possible families:** documentary/editorial, cinematic threshold, graphic/poster system. These are **exercise scaffolds**, not required outputs or default chosen style. **Failure:** art direction only changes cosmetics or copies observed artworks. **Check:** each concept can be described in two materially different sentences without naming its palette.

## AD08 — Critique at two distances, with real evidence
**When:** designers or AI declare an untested render a masterpiece. **Decision:** near-range craft review (crop, typography, spacing, responsiveness, actual links, keyboard, contrast, loading) and far-range semantic review (truth, conceptual originality, cultural fit, aesthetic restraint). Bring independent criticism when practical. **Example:** contrast visually seductive inaccessible diner in Hopper (MA-003) with a functional digital doorway: **don't transfer artistic exclusion to a UI**. **Failure:** one synthetic quality score hides disastrous usability or an unoriginal imitation. **Check:** actual screenshot/interaction at 320/390/768/1440; label not-tested axes, record what changed.

## Two coordinated visual operating modes

| Dimension | Expressive — marketing / public storytelling | Utility — operations / consequential tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Emotion, identity, audience relevance, meaningful invitation | Speed, accurate state, safe completion, recovery |
| Layout | Editorial variation; deliberately framed imagery and type | Predictable information hierarchy, robust component states |
| Motion | Purposeful accents/sequence, always accessible bypass | Immediate, restrained, no gatekeeping |
| Photography | Rights-cleared, subject-specific, contextual | Only when it clarifies object/task; never distracts |
| Typography | Expressive *display* language with readable action layer | Legible UI text, semantic labels and scale |
| Shared identity | Same product truth, logo, tone, color roles and type family | Same identity, quieter role-specific presentation |

Neither mode excuses inaccessible contrast, invented product information or high GPU cost. Each user journey may contain both, e.g. an emotionally rich public profile beside a precise check-in button.

## Art director → UX → engineering handoff
`verified audience/job → actual product truth → expressive/utility mix → chosen mechanism(s) + linked original source → creative thesis → 3 alternative premises → locked content/CTA and rights → visual grammar → UX accessibility/trust invariants → responsive/photo/motion notes → original prototype evidence → independent critique → next revision`.
