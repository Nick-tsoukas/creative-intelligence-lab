# Architecture → UX: An Art Direction Card

**Use during creative briefing or critique, not frontend implementation.** Source: [Research 010](../research/010-ARCHITECTURE-OF-DIGITAL-EXPERIENCE.md).

## Six prompts
1. **Arrival:** What can a new visitor understand immediately?
2. **Circulation:** Which path tells the story? Which path lets a task-focused visitor skip it?
3. **Focal event:** What one piece of evidence deserves peak visual emphasis?
4. **Threshold:** Where should the emotional atmosphere change, and why?
5. **Material:** How does typography, imagery and light reinforce the concept rather than mimic an architect's signature?
6. **Return:** Can a visitor revisit an earlier section without penalty?

## Warnings
- Architecture's imposed physical route is not a justification for scrolljacking.
- A dramatic dark/bright scheme is not a substitute for accessible contrast.
- A photograph of a building is not evidence that a webpage's interaction will work.
- Use architectural principles as reasoning inputs, **never copy a protected architect's distinctive works**.

## Handoff output
Specify subject, one-sentence experiential thesis, information hierarchy, story path, skip path, mechanism and counterexample, rights-cleared imagery needs, and ways to test comprehension. Hand off to a frontend implementer without micromanaging CSS or component architecture.
