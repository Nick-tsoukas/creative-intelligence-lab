# Cinematic Direction Card — Working v0.1

Use this short checklist in creative briefs. For evidence and caveats read [Research 007](../research/007-CINEMATIC-IMAGE-LANGUAGE.md).

## Frame
- What is the image's true subject?
- Which crop, lighting and viewpoint communicate that subject?
- What should remain stable across a series, and where may the frame vary?
- Is the subject legible on mobile, under overlays, and with text nearby?

## Sequence
- What information must be known **immediately**?
- What deserves a dramatic moment, and what should remain quiet?
- How do timing, stillness, reveal and feedback differ?
- Can the user skip, interrupt, reverse or revisit the sequence?

## Material
- Are photography, illustrations, typography, texture, sound and motion consistent with the *real subject*?
- Is there authentic content or authorized imagery available?
- Does realism communicate the truth of the product rather than staged generic aspiration?

## Behavior and accessibility
- No false playback cues, invisible navigation, forced auto-play, or unnecessary scroll traps.
- Respect reduced motion and meaningful user control.
- Never use cinematic effects to hide forms, pricing, actions or important content.
- Record exact browser/viewport evidence when tested.

## One-question critique
**Would the page still communicate its promise if we turned off every animation and removed decorative textures?** If not, revise the information architecture first.
