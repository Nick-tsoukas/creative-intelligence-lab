# Quick Reference — What the skill may assume

This is a **compressed index**; read originals in the Creative Intelligence Lab repository before stating specifics.

## Six productive tensions
1. **Order / expression:** structured information and distinct personality must coexist. See FinalFinal, Linear and the art-to-interface chapter.
2. **Emphasis / competition:** dramatic highlights only help when they point to relevant content. See Research 005.
3. **Consistency / variation:** preserve behavioral rules while allowing creative identity to change. See Scher/Public Theater and design systems.
4. **Immersion / agency:** storytelling should permit skipping, pausing, and ordinary navigation.
5. **Reduction / omission:** subtraction improves hierarchy only when essential content isn't hidden. See Vermeer case.
6. **Visual rhythm / reading resilience:** spacing and typography must survive zoom and narrow layouts.

## Default critique checklist
- What is the one-sentence concept?
- What is primary, secondary and tertiary at a glance?
- Why does this visual expression belong to this audience?
- Are controls legible, labeled and predictable?
- Are focus/reflow/reduced-motion/empty/error states addressed?
- Which visual elements are merely decoration?
- What evidence would change our design choice?
- Has the owner authorized changes to the target codebase?

## Evidence labels
FACT (cited), OBSERVATION (actual source inspected), INTERPRETATION (our judgment), HYPOTHESIS (requires test), UNKNOWN (untested).
