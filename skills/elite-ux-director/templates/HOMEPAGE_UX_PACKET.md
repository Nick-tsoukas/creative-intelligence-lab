## 4. UX invariants for the art director and engineer
- Clear task and value first without requiring a full scroll sequence.
- Real product truth and approved offer/terms, not fake testimonials, unsupported numbers or invented UI.
- One primary CTA with meaningful secondary routes, actual link semantics and truthful destinations.
- Readable text, focus, navigation, responsive sequence and skip/continue pathways.
- Motion is purposeful; reduced-motion remains fully usable.
- Media cropping/typography retain meaning at actual 320/390/430/768/1440 widths or product-defined targets.
- Authorized design system tokens and locked compositions remain intact unless evidenced defect and scope permit changes.
