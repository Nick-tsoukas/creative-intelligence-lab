# Creative reasoning, worked premises and design memory

Load for an unresolved visual premise or consequential rendered critique. These
are decision tools, not a second universal checklist. Worked examples below are
transfer hypotheses; the Night example has field observations, not a controlled
study or owner acceptance. Adapt the mechanism, not the visual identity.

## Turn content into composition

Start with the actual content and relationship: what deserves attention, what
must be compared, what should be felt, and what must remain available? Identify
one organizing relationship before choosing surfaces or effects.

- **Weight and hierarchy:** name the first, second and third focal events. Compare the masses of type, images and controls; a small high-contrast action can outweigh a large quiet image. Reserve dominance for the task/meaning that earns it. Equal choices can legitimately share equal weight.
- **Space and rhythm:** use proximity to make belonging clear, and changes of density/scale to mark chapters or decisions. A gap should separate meanings, not simply make the page longer. Test whether removing a container preserves grouping and affordance; cards are useful when their boundaries mean something.
- **Type as a role:** decide whether type announces, narrates, labels or enables comparison. Try the longest real title, a dense paragraph, numbers and labels in the actual font before fixing scale/width. Match line length, leading and wrap rhythm to that role. Use the approved family/system first; a new typeface needs a reason and authorized scope, not a generic desire to look different.
- **Images as structure:** inspect actual subject, viewpoint, usable resolution, rights and focal space before choosing geometry. Try representative and difficult assets: portrait/landscape, crowded background, weakest legitimate photo and no-photo state. A layout that works only with an ideal placeholder has not demonstrated its premise.
- **Accessible mobile transformation:** preserve meaningful reading/focus order; choose what leads when columns become a sequence. Keep utility type and actions perceivable. Uncropped imagery, reflow and reduced motion can strengthen the idea; they are design inputs, not late compliance decoration.

Describe each concept as **organizing idea → intended impression → task benefit
→ sacrifice**. Explore rough hierarchy/sequence first; build only the fidelity
needed for the owner's decision. More implementations do not necessarily yield
more useful alternatives.

## Three worked examples

### Performance memory: two ways to remember the same Night

**Relationship/content:** an artist and the people who shared a performance;
real concert photographs, public writing, galleries and private band notes.

**A — Cinematic Night:** performance image as arrival → immediate recollection
→ artist recognizes and relives this particular Night before completing it
→ dependence on photo quality/crop and a longer opening. Large artist identity
balances the performer; compact dateline/actions yield to the image, then Story
and a bounded photographic chapter. On mobile, choose a subject-safe crop and
bring writing/actions into a clear subsequent sequence. A type-only fallback
must still carry identity. Do not transfer this large opening to urgent show
operations where status and check-in controls must be immediately available.

**B — Living Music Journal:** venue/date and writing as an editorial spread
→ personal record → calm reading and authorship
→ less immediate spectacle; in the observed prototype the print preceded Story
on mobile, moving writing lower. An uncropped portrait faces the prose on desktop;
mobile must choose explicitly whether portrait or writing leads. Choosing writing
first would be a different tradeoff, not a free improvement. Avoid this leisurely
sequence for a short urgent task. Neither premise is superior merely because its
behavior checks pass; owner preference and user response remain separate evidence.

### Payment review: precision as the visual character

**Relationship/content:** a person deciding whether to approve a consequential
payment; payee identity, amount, account, timing and review action.

**Premise:** an aligned review ledger → deliberate confidence → compare the
few consequential facts before confirming → less expressive arrival and lower
information density than an expert transaction table. Give amount and payee
coherent weight; align numeric comparisons, label units/status unambiguously and
reserve a distinct area for the final consequence/action. Brand can live in type
rhythm and fine structure without obscuring these facts. Mobile becomes one
meaningful reading order with paired labels/values, not squeezed desktop columns.
A large decorative photo would compete with trust. Conversely, this ledger is
inappropriate as a default for a music memory or an editorial feature: its
comparison structure would flatten the subject's emotional relationships.

### Editorial archive: chronology that makes change visible

**Relationship/content:** a reader following a place through dated field notes,
uneven photographs and gaps in the record.

**Premise:** dated chapters with evidence beside interpretation → accumulated
history → understand what changed and what remains unknown → slower random access
than a dense catalog. Use date/type scale and repeated alignments as the stable
grammar; vary image size only when evidence warrants attention. Keep absence legible
without decorative empty cards. Mobile follows the same chronology with compact
jump navigation; captions stay attached to their evidence. An atmospheric full
screen can undermine comparison. This chapter model is inappropriate for urgent
monitoring or an expert trying to compare many records simultaneously; there a
stable table/list may be the distinctive, correct choice.

## Critique that changes a decision

State two short conclusions, with evidence appropriate to each:

- **Reliability:** what can people understand, complete, recover and trust? Behavioral tests support tested contracts; screenshots support visible claims. Neither proves what real users feel.
- **Creative quality:** name the strongest content-specific relationship and the weakest design decision. Cite the actual type/image/space relationship; “premium” or “generic” alone is not a diagnosis.

Before changing the render, predict a concrete effect: “reduce the competing
heading so the photograph and venue become the opening pair.” Then inspect the
affected state. Avoid simultaneous aesthetic changes that make their effect
impossible to judge. A convention is not automatically generic; ask whether it
serves the content/job. Originality that hides essential meaning is a failure.
Distinguish self-review, independent critique and owner taste; an unresolved owner
choice can finish as a decision-ready comparison.

## Research and learning without another system

When a visual uncertainty persists, inspect a few relevant real source images:
`question → observed mechanism → conditions/counterexample → original transfer`.
A named inspiration site or a saved thumbnail is not proof the mechanism fits.
Use [research routing](RESEARCH_AND_DISTILLATION.md); the Lab's existing Atlas,
packet router and private visual workspace are optional, never standalone package
dependencies. Preserve rights and source-access limits; do not imitate a
recognizable identity.

Reuse the project's decision record. After meaningful authorized work, a brief
addition can note the decision the skill changed, real defect discovered, work
caused/prevented, actual owner/user verdict, and whether quality improved or is
still unknown. Record confounds such as project guidance, engineering and other
reviewers. Preserve rejected premises and later reversals; don't universalize one
owner preference. No retrospective is required for every small repair.
