# Elite UX Director — Core Source Ledger v0.3

**Scope:** source support for general UX decision cards, not a claim that entire books have been read. Full scholarly notes remain in the Creative Intelligence Lab's `reading-dossiers/` and source archives; an installed skill should not need the repository to follow its general operating algorithm.

## Direct primary texts and exact reading limits

| Source | Access / original text | Actually examined | Supported transferable mechanism | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jeremy Keith, *Resilient Web Design* (2016) | https://resilientwebdesign.com/ (author full book, CC BY-SA) | Chapters 5 Layers, 6 Steps | Core meaningful user verbs and progressive enhancement under failures | Not an empirical guarantee that any JavaScript-free application exists |
| Patrick J. | Hornbæk, Kristensson & Oulasvirta, *Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction* (2025) | https://pokristensson.com/pubs/IntroductionToHumanComputerInteraction.pdf (coauthor OA PDF) | Intro, portions of Ch1 and Ch3 pp 37–40, 49–57 | Distinguish search, detection, discrimination, recognition, attention, prior expectations | Full text available does not mean fully read; PDF diagrams not fully visually verified |
| Brad Frost, *Atomic Design* (2016) | https://atomicdesign.bradfrost.com/chapter-2/ | Chapter 2 directly read | Component/template/page feedback, test real copy/content variation | Atomic hierarchy alone does not establish task success or privacy semantics |
| Massimo Vignelli, *The Vignelli Canon* (2009) | https://www.rit.edu/vignellicenter/sites/rit.edu.vignellicenter/files/documents/The%20Vignelli%20Canon.pdf | Selected Semantics, Syntactics, Ambiguity, Letterhead and type relationships spreads | Meaning and design grammar precede surface layout | Artistic philosophy; not empirical evidence of UX superiority |
| Sasha Costanza-Chock, *Design Justice* (2020) | https://designjustice.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/cfohnud7/release/4 | Selected design-practices chapter sections | Participation, accountability, caution about fabricated personas | Normative framework; no claims about every user's preferences |
| William James, *Principles of Psychology*, Vol I (1890; 1918 digitized edition) | https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/57628 | Selected Ch IV Habit and XI Attention passages | Historical questions about attention and learned habits | Historical hypotheses do not establish contemporary neuroscience |

## Author articles and frameworks, distinct from books
- Heydon Pickering, [Notifications](https://inclusive-components.design/notifications/) and [Tabbed Interfaces](https://inclusive-components.design/tabbed-interfaces/): **articles read**, the separately sold book **not read**. Nonintrusive announcements, focus, tabs versus router links. Check current platform support.
- Jakob Nielsen, [10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) and [How to Conduct a Heuristic Evaluation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-conduct-a-heuristic-evaluation/): **articles read**, not Nielsen's copyrighted books. Diagnostic heuristics, not participant observations.

## Technical standards (not books)
- [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) — accessibility normative criteria; no conformance claimed.
- [WAI-ARIA APG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/) — implementation patterns; verify actual code and keyboard/screen reader outcomes.
- [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/) — strong official pattern guidance, not automatically applicable in every context.

## Reading and decision rules
**Full-text link verified** ≠ **acquired** ≠ **selected chapter read** ≠ **entire book fully read** ≠ **validated in users**. These labels must never be collapsed.

Read one or two relevant cards via `scripts/retrieve.py`, inspect this ledger only when making a substantive source claim, and return to current product code/behavior. Citations are not permissions, not backend behavior, and not guaranteed UX outcomes.
