## Four high-value research sprints (each creates a different output)
1. **Deep pixels + primary attribution.** Choose 3 of existing MA cards, obtain a legitimate high-quality original display, verify exact work and photographer rights, analyze full visual frame at original ratio, compare institution commentary, update `DEEP_VISUAL_STUDY` only when genuinely done. Prioritize Vermeer, Hokusai, Albers. Record disputed interpretations instead of one authority.
2. **Cinematography / performance sequencing.** Research specific legally accessible film or concert sequences with shot-by-shot notes: image field, camera vantage, key/fill/edge light, duration/cut, subject truth, pacing and audience context; no copyrighted video archives duplicated. Deliver 3 source-verified dossiers and one original storyboard exercise. Distinguish actual motion from screenshots and avoid inventing a director's intent.
3. **Music cultural grammar.** Examine 3 verified actual Blue Note/other independent-label sleeve systems and 3 contemporary authorized musician campaigns; identify coherent recurring identity rules across editions vs individual band authorship. Review licensing, credit, typography, display, responsive analogues. Avoid letting the jazz aesthetic define every genre.
4. **Contemporary websites in the browser.** Study at least 3 *real*, active expressive digital properties on mobile and desktop: navigation, page purpose, keyboard, reduced motion, hero media failure, rendering performance where measured, access to actual CTA. Do not assume awards signal usability. Document screenshot provenance and rights.
