# Creative Intelligence Lab — Master Product Plan to v1.0

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** Canonical strategic execution plan, owner vision accepted; individual engineering/design milestones remain subject to evidence and explicit authorization where noted.  
**Primary application:** Faneqo, a music-industry artist/fan web/mobile product.  
**Secondary application:** Any web/mobile product through an adapter and product-specific brief.  
**Owner's aspiration:** breathtaking, world-class art direction AND exceptional product experience. This is a target, not a claim of superiority over other studios.

## 0. Product thesis

The Lab is not a moodboard, theme generator, exhaustive museum, or autonomous production code writer. It is a **research-grounded Creative Direction System** that turns verified cultural context and artistic knowledge into original directions, usable interface specifications, tested prototypes and iterative independent critique.

The repeatable system is:

**Understand → Research → Artistic thesis → Three distinct concepts → Owner selection → Design/build handoff → Isolated implementation → Evidence → Independent critique → Iteration → Reusable learning.**

The system must earn its value by producing more distinctive, emotionally apt, usable work than an ordinary frontend-design workflow given the *same* brief, constraints, content and implementation effort. It may fail that test. If it does, revise or remove unnecessary complexity.

### Output promise and guardrails

- First-class output: art direction + deep reasoning + actual implementable specifications + iterative critique.
- Final output: distinctive, responsive, keyboard-usable web/mobile experiences; measured results when feasible.
- Strong aesthetic quality cannot be certified through numerical checklist scores alone. Founder and independent reviewers judge artistic significance separately from task success.
- Never copy distinctive copyrighted artwork or clone a specific studio/site. Reference mechanics and sources, not unlicensed assets.
- Avoid production Faneqo edits until explicitly authorized. Sandbox design studies are not deployment approvals.
- No background autonomy claims. Work occurs in the current active session or separately authorized automation.
- Treat Faneqo product facts as a *freshly verified input* at pilot time, not permanently cached design specifications.

## 1. Architecture — five products inside one Lab

### A. Evidence Library
Curated primary writings, significant artworks, original artist/designer interviews, architecture, visual communication, digital product case studies, exemplary accessibility patterns and technical references. Versioned source status (FULLY READ vs EXCERPT REVIEWED etc), origin, publication/date, legally usable rights and observed limitations.

### B. Artistic Reasoning Engine
Compact conditional principles: composition, balance, typography, image direction, cinematic rhythm, emotional sequencing, color, identity, interaction, attention and perception. Every principle names its source mechanism, where it works, counterexamples, and what would falsify the recommendation. Avoid universal golden-ratio/eye-tracking myths.

### C. Creative Director Skill
Selective retrieval of 2–4 references relevant to a real brief; writes a thesis; develops 3 *conceptually distinct* directions; compares authenticity, expressive quality, comprehension, accessibility, performance and production complexity. Produces an implementation-neutral direction packet. Complements rather than duplicates Claude/Anthropic frontend-design. Existing alpha requires validation.

### D. Design Engineering & Evidence
An implementer (Claude or other) creates isolated, testable Nuxt/Vue/HTML prototypes using verified product requirements; Playwright/manual browser captures, responsive checks, keyboard/focus, reduced motion, navigation, forms/error states, technical performance and SSR/hydration tests where applicable. Preserve explicit observed-versus-unverified status. Engineering works in a separate sandbox unless owner authorizes production scope.

### E. Critique & Learning System
Independent artistic critique, anti-generic audit, task-based usability and accessibility review, screenshot evidence, design decision history, before/after comparisons and source-linked revised principles. A critique must specify *what to change and why*. Each accepted solution becomes a reusable design mechanism, not copied project-specific styling.

## 2. Domain and reuse architecture

**Core:** general art/design evidence, artistic mechanisms, creative direction, UX/accessibility, performance, collaboration and critique.

**Faneqo adapter:** musicians, touring culture, creators, fans, event energy, authentic artist photography, trust, ticketless interactions, artist profiles, Show Mode, conversion, trial/paid messaging, audience differences, branding and the *actual verified app* at pilot time. Music should be experienced as culture and identity, not stock neon, waveform motifs or faux festival posters.

**Other domain adapters:** each supplies audience, product truths, source material, constraints, visual identity, business objectives, content, and target platforms. No hard-coded Faneqo assumptions in the core skill.

**Boundary:** Research/design repo is independent from live frontend/backend repositories. No payment/billing/security changes are within Creative Intelligence scope.

**Exit:** owner accepts or rejects a demonstrably functioning art-directed sandbox, with screenshots/test evidence and documented tradeoffs.

## 5. Quality and acceptance gates

### Artistic excellence (qualitative)
- A clear original central idea and coherent visual language.
- Strong hierarchy, rhythm, composition, typographic voice, color relationships and image direction.
- Authentic cultural specificity — real music-industry meaning, not interchangeable generic SaaS decoration.
- Deliberate emotional pacing, surprise and restraint, not maximal effects everywhere.
- Expert/owner critique with demonstrated revisions; aesthetic quality judged qualitatively, not as pass/fail from a score alone.

### Product/UX
- The primary task and key product explanation remain discoverable; users can skip narrative and navigate.
- Controls and text are legible at 320, 390, tablet and desktop widths.
- Keyboard/focus/labels, reduced motion, meaningful errors, empty states, and good reflow are tested.
- No unjustified scrolljacking, deceptive animations or decorative complexity preventing action.

### Engineering
- Fast, resilient interactions. Measure LCP/CLS/INP where real instrumentation is available; no made-up metrics.
- Maintainable Nuxt 3 Composition API patterns; SSR/hydration verified for applicable builds.
- Accessible semantic structure, optimized images, stable motion and no needless dependencies.
- Repeatable browser test instructions and screenshots with provenance.

### Scientific/evidence honesty
- Sources label what was read/observed, date, primary/secondary, exact scope, copyright rights and uncertainties.
- Experiments identify uncontrolled variables and cannot be described as proven causal effects.
- Prototype QA distinguishes written tests, executed tests and human-observed results.

**Nonnegotiable release blocks:** broken core task, serious keyboard/reading-order barrier, copyrighted reference copied into product without rights, unapproved production edit, claimed tests not actually run.

## 7. Open risks and deliberate tradeoffs

- **Research bloat:** mitigate by tying additions to a decision, experiment or case-study gate.
- **Beauty without usable product:** independently test task completion, accessibility and meaningful content.
- **AI aesthetic convergence:** reject predictable interchangeable 'cinematic SaaS' motifs; require culturally specific thesis and distinct options.
- **Music-industry authenticity:** source real artist/fan experiences, music photography/identity, rights, constraints and context.
- **Unproven alpha skill:** evaluate against a baseline; if it adds no meaningful value, simplify rather than defend it.
- **Restricted agent availability:** continue independent research and browser QA; no diversion of Claude from active engineering without owner instruction.
- **Legal/media rights:** link research art; use licensed/user-owned assets in shipped implementations.
- **Premature production integration:** isolated mockups until owner review, not implicit authorization.
