Modified/rejected: three modes are a selector, not three new mandatory processes;
prototype boundaries are recorded once, not excuse labels for false local success.
No universal style bans, beauty scores, required new personas, compulsory research
packet, extra orchestration or large benchmark program. Existing libraries remain
available without entering routine repair context. The early creative-director
alpha keeps its own research role; this update does not overwrite it or v5.0.

Additional creative improvements: test the organizing idea against weak/absent
real assets before polishing; make typography serve announcing/narrating/labeling/
comparison; choose mobile sequence as an explicit tradeoff; predict the effect
of the single highest-leverage correction before editing. These are reusable
judgment mechanisms with counterexamples, not claims of measured superiority.
