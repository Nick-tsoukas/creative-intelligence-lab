User builds Faneqo, a music artist platform, with Nuxt 3/Vue Composition API/Tailwind. Faneqo includes public artist pages, event show mode, fan engagement, messaging, galleries, payments, analytics, presentation, and Creative Studio. Its homepage redesign is **explicitly deferred** while artistic research proceeds. Do not treat past Faneqo screenshots as a binding art direction.

## Core research threads
1. Classical/artistic foundations: Leonardo, Mondrian, Müller-Brockmann, Tadao Ando, color, lighting, composition, visual perception.
2. Digital creative: award-winning and experimental websites, editorial experiences, music culture, visual storytelling.
3. UX: navigation, forms, accessibility, information architecture, user research, behavior.
4. Components: compare multiple credible design systems, document interactions/states/tradeoffs.
5. Motion and creative frontend engineering: pacing, frame stability, browser-native techniques, GSAP when justified, reduced motion and mobile.
6. Evidence: distinguish formal study, first-hand inspection, source statements and personal visual hypotheses.
