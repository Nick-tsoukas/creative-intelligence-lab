Instead:

- added `handbook/MASTERPIECE_ART_DIRECTION_DECISIONS.md`, eight conditional reasoning moves (AD01–AD08); and `templates/MASTERPIECE_DIRECTION_PACKET.md`, a three-distinct-directions output packet and independent critique;

## Known limitations and next gates
- All eight studies are **first-pass preview-observed** with institutional/creator context, not full-resolution artwork exams, museum visits, fine-art research monographs, photography-rights grants or human usability studies.
- Audio/music and film still need **actual scene-by-scene visual analysis**, not just listed links. Digital art-direction sites need live browser/interactive/mobile investigation, not award screenshots.
- `v5.0` is a label from the handoff, not a checked-out source tag available here; avoid falsely claiming this branch upgraded the installed v5.0.
- No formal standalone skill benchmark run: owner's direction is to learn organically during authorized projects. Static CI does not prove aesthetic excellence or measurable UX improvements.

**Conclusion:** A compatible, tested-for-structure *research module* can be merged without corrupting unknown v5.0. Actual v5.0 integration must happen at its verifiable source checkout, not by overwriting v0.x entrypoints.
