## Observed photograph (limited)
A dark concrete chapel reads as an almost rectangular enclosure, with strong wall planes and repeating wooden benches toward the front. A cross-shaped luminous opening dominates the back/altar wall. The photograph includes a seated person; the strongest visible light is concentrated at that front cruciform opening rather than spread evenly through the room. This is an **editorial photo of the building**, not a first-hand walkthrough.

## Creator says
Ando's office describes aiming for a **primary stripped-back space** under tight site/budget constraints, and a simple concrete chamber transformed through its cross opening and natural light. The architectural sequence includes a dividing wall and sense of entry. The site and religious use are integral, not ornamental metaphors.

## Interpretation / alternate
**Hypothesis:** meaning can intensify through a deliberately restrained sequence of spaces and a single strong reveal. **Alternate:** a physical devotional site is meant to be inhabited and lingered in; forcing online visitors through darkness, delay or a disorienting navigation threshold would misapply the work.

## Transferable digital mechanism
**Use when:** a storytelling chapter can build anticipation then reveal a genuine product/user result while maintaining immediate navigation and bypass.

**Don't use when:** designing a modal, message composer, loading screen, fan check-in, signup form, or screen where urgent action requires immediate clarity; "make the visitor wait" is not a transfer principle.

## Original exercise
Storyboarding exercise with **the same verified content and destination**: one straightforward editorial section and one spatially sequenced reveal. Both must provide links and essential content immediately, support reduced motion, remain readable with no image, and show a functioning path to the intended action. Compare narrative impact separately from task completion, performance and accessibility.
