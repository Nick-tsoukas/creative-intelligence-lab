# MA-001 — Quiet emphasis: Vermeer, *Woman Holding a Balance*

- **Creator / date / medium:** Johannes Vermeer, c. 1664, oil on canvas.
- **Primary object:** National Gallery of Art, Washington, Widener Collection, accession 1942.9.97: https://www.nga.gov/artworks/1236-woman-holding-balance
- **Institutional close reading:** https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/dutch-paintings-seventeenth-century-woman-holding-balance-c
- **Image evidence:** low-resolution third-party preview of the attributed NGA work visibly inspected on 2026-11-15; **not** full-resolution painting, pigment study or original museum visit. Institution object record and curator account separately read.
- **Rights:** artwork image available via NGA object record, check the precise supplied image/license for reproduction; don't copy the preview from unrelated sites into a product.

## Observed preview (limited)
Vertical interior scene. The brightly lit white head covering, blue jacket and exposed hand form a quiet cluster to the right of a darker draped mass at left. A window is suggested at the upper left. The hand holds a delicate balance in front of the torso. Darker framed imagery occupies the high-right backdrop. The person has visual importance **without a large bright silhouette taking over the whole frame**.

## Institution says
NGA's scholarly entry situates the woman's gesture against a background Last Judgment and explains how the hanging scales, painted frame, light and space support multiple possible themes of weighing and reflection. The curator notes subtle visual axes and a balance of mass and void. **These iconographic readings are scholarly interpretations, not facts derived simply from the preview.**

## Interpretation / alternative
**Hypothesis:** local light and careful spatial relation can prioritize a quiet action amid richness. **Alternative:** the painting's meaning may depend more on historical iconography and domestic social context than on the perceived focal path of a modern visitor.
