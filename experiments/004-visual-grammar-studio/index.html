01 / THE CONCEPT

One message.
Three different visual arguments.

Imagine a fictional creative service that gives a performance an afterlife. Every design above has the same headline, promise and destination. The difference is how hierarchy, space and expressive language shape first impressions.

This demonstration does not offer signup, publish artist accounts, or represent real musicians. The change-of-style controls are a comparison exercise, not a feature recommendation.
