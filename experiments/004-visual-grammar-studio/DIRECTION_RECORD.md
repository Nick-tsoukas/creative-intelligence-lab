# Design Decision Record — Visual Grammar Studio

**Brief:** Fictional independent-music experience, “Encore Notes”; demonstrate a single “show → memory → reconnect” promise with legible link to a concept description. No real platform behavior, bookings, signup, fans or photos.

**Fixed content across modes:** headline “The night stays with you.” Supporting proposition, “A home for the moments after the music stops.” Explicit utility text and in-page “Explore the concept” CTA, actual anchor, short explanation below. Same information, no invented artist photograph or financial outcomes.

**Creative constraints:** different visual thesis and reading rhythm, not merely three recolors; all links semantic; 320px reflow, focus indication and motion preference; safe no-JS default; source-driven but no recognizable artwork copies. Compatible with 8 GB laptop (no build tools, fonts, network or third-party images).

**Editorial:** *The printed setlist as artifact.* Large stable typographic title, warm paper geometry, small label and quiet editorial marginalia. Risk: too flat/nostalgic. Visual mechanism: local focal weight and careful subordination (Dow, Vermeer source-reading analogy, not copied composition).

**Cinema:** *The audience sees the stage afterglow.* Original layered radial lighting and curve silhouettes, headline on the low band, supporting promise and plain CTA. Risk: atmospheric title/CTA disappear on dark media or animation; no essential content depends on effects. Analogies to Hopper light/threshold and Ando focused reveal are explicitly bounded—no simulated blocked doorway/forced path.

**Signal:** *The show leaves a mark.* Stacked display type, angular bands and visual punctuation; copy/CTA retained as readable human interface. Risk: breakpoints/long word-wrap turn the poster into illegible fragments. Analogies to Scher's expressive-utility distinction and Hokusai dramatic vs stable scale; no recognizable graphic/wordmark copied.

**Unresolved, cannot be decided by design alone:** real music audiences, cultural/genre fit, authentic photographic subject selection, human comprehension or aesthetic preference. The fictional demo does not substitute for Faneqo current visuals or an owner's direction approval.

**What to review with actual screenshots:** at 320/390/768/1440, large text and text-spacing overrides, focus, meaning of active mode, CTA click, no media, no JS, reduced motion. Document actual findings and fixes in a future test report; don't turn code-structure checks into visual QA.
