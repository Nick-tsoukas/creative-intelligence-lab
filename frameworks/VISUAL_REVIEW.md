# Visual Experience Review Framework

## Scope
Evaluate **visual art** and **functional UX** separately, then analyze the tension between them.

## Dimensions (1–5 subjective diagnostic scores)
Composition, hierarchy, typography, color/light, imagery, emotional resonance, motion, clarity, craft, usability/accessibility.

## Questions
1. What first attracts attention and why?
2. What is the intended message and action? Is the hierarchy aligned?
3. Which precise relationships between sizes, shapes, intervals and colors create its character?
4. What does the motion communicate? Is reduced motion respected?
5. What happens on narrow screens and slow devices? Have you actually inspected?
6. Are interactive components discoverable, keyboard reachable, reversible, and accessible?
7. What would be lost if the strongest visual effect were removed?
8. Which influences are structural (transferable) versus stylistic (not to copy)?
9. Can you name a counterexample in which the same technique fails?

## Written review output
Document strengths with concrete evidence, weaknesses with severity, ambiguities, constraints, applicable sources, and experiments. Scores are *not* objective measures of beauty and should never override clear evidence.
